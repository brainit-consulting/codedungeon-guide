    Chapter X

    Keys at a glance

    Every key the dungeon listens to. None of them act while you are typing in a box.

    W A S D or arrowsWalk
    ShiftRun
    MouseLook around (click the view first)
    Mouse wheelZoom in and out; middle click to zoom back out
    E or left clickUse what the cross is on
    EscFree the mouse, or close a panel
    PYour scroll
    TabShow or hide who's working
    HHelp
    BThis User Guide
    CCall the cat
    FThrow a dart (hold for a harder throw)
    GPut the darts back
    MAll sound on or off
    NMusic on or off
    EnterSend, in the scroll and in a terminal's message box
    Shift + EnterNew line in those boxes

    In this book, ← and → turn to the previous and next chapter.
